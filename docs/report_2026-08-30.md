# FLNC experiment report — 2026-08-30

Semantic views for CG3 at few labels: baseline validation (V1), encoder/view
ablation (FEW-30), and fusion mechanism × heterophily + anomaly probes
(FEW-31). All runs: Modal A10G, 10 seeds, paired per-seed 95% CIs, frozen
protocol (budget 20/class unless noted, epochs 200, patience 200, fixed
shared hyperparameters — no per-dataset tuning). Both experiments were
preregistered before submission (`docs/hypotheses.md`,
`docs/hypotheses_few31.md` on `scaffold/data-and-experiment`); predictions
were never edited after data. Code: `71b3c583`. Data: release `data-v1`
(sha256-pinned) + the six heterophilic TAG bundles.

## 1. Is the baseline trustworthy? (V1 — yes, with a disclosed gap)

New pipeline A0 ≡ faithful CG3 port at identical settings (0.7890 ± 0.0122 vs
0.7853 ± 0.0127 on cora), and 1000 epochs changes nothing (0.7871 ± 0.0127).
The ~4.5-pt gap to published CG3 (83.4) is per-dataset validation tuning of
the loss weights, which we deliberately do not do: all claims are paired
arm differences under identical shared dials. The structural-only control
carries zero semantic parameters (verified; a prior version leaked a
trainable class-prior branch into the control and was fixed before any run).

## 2. Does the semantic view help? It depends on the graph (FEW-30)

Δ accuracy vs A0 (pts, paired 95% CI), A0 = cora 0.789 / citeseer_tag 0.694 /
pubmed 0.792:

| arm (concat fusion) | cora | citeseer_tag | pubmed |
|---|---|---|---|
| sbert (own text, MiniLM 384d) | **−3.6** [−4.2,−3.0] | +1.2 [+0.1,+2.4] | −1.6 [−2.2,−0.9] |
| e5-large (1024d) | **−7.1** [−7.8,−6.3] | +0.5 [−0.4,+1.5] | −0.0 [−0.8,+0.8] |
| gpt3l (3072d) | −0.5 [−1.6,+0.5] | +1.4 [+0.4,+2.4] | **+3.9** [+3.4,+4.5] |
| tape_stripped (LLM explanations) | −5.8 | +2.4 | **+10.4** ⚠ leakage |
| tape_full (unstripped) | −4.4 | +2.2 | +10.9 ⚠ |

- The view **hurts where structure is strong** (cora), mildly helps
  citeseer_tag, and only the strongest encoder helps pubmed. Encoder
  "capacity ordering" fails: e5 is the worst arm on cora.
- **Leakage (probe-confirmed, §4):** pubmed's explanation gain is the LLM
  answering the task inside the explanation. Do not headline.
- H3 (declarative leak) confirmed on cora: unstripped − stripped = +1.32,
  vs the prior +1.36.

## 3. The fusion mechanism (FEW-31 F/S)

The HSIC gate is degenerate (realized HSIC ≈ 0.01 ≪ τ = 0.1): every FEW-30
result is concat fusion; entropy attention had never executed. Forcing the
paths (gpt3l view, Δ vs A0):

| fusion | cora | citeseer_tag | pubmed |
|---|---|---|---|
| concat | −0.5 | +1.4 | **+3.9** |
| entropy attention | −1.9 | +0.1 | +2.7 |
| shared_private v1 (AM-GCN placement, CKA) | −7.8 ± 3.0 | −3.3 | +0.3 |

- **Concat wins everywhere tested.** Attention never beats it (F1 refuted,
  F1b confirmed) — including on the low-homophily graphs (G3 refuted: 1/4).
- **shared_private v1 underperforms with 2–3× seed variance** (S1/S2/S3
  refuted). The placement critique it implements remains valid in principle;
  v1's execution needs a post-mortem (suspects: 7→64-dim structural
  projection, consistency weight 1.0). Diagnostics (loss_consist, loss_disp)
  are in MLflow.
- The cross-view HSIC **penalty is a verified no-op** (w=0 vs w=0.1:
  +0.08 ± 0.24 pts) — FEW-30 is unconfounded by the placement critique.

## 4. Anomaly probes (graph-free logistic regression, 20/class)

- **e5's −7.1 is mostly a fusion artifact:** as a bare embedding space e5
  trails sbert by only 2.0 pts on cora (0.727 vs 0.747).
- **pubmed leakage confirmed:** stripped-explanation embeddings alone score
  0.877 (no graph; trained model: 0.895), +4.4 over the raw-text probe.
  Declaration-stripping does not remove leakage carried by meaning.
- Unregistered but striking: probes nearly match the trained pipeline
  everywhere (cora gpt3l probe 0.777 vs full CG3 0.789) — at these budgets,
  frozen text embeddings + logistic regression are close to competitive
  with the whole graph model.

## 5. The heterophily axis (FEW-31 G) — with a failed validity check

**V1 fails on all six heterophilic graphs: plain CG3 is below the
majority-class rate everywhere** (texas 0.454 vs 0.541, actor 0.380 vs
0.488, …). CG3's components all assume homophily; it is the wrong backbone
there. G-verdicts are therefore provisional — the semantic view *rescues a
failing baseline* (crossing the majority bar on cornell, wisconsin,
washington) rather than improving a working one.

Δ (sbert view, concat) vs A0: cornell +8.9 ± 3.5, wisconsin +9.8 ± 6.1,
actor +8.0 ± 8.3, washington +4.4 ± 6.9, amazon_ratings +1.1 ± 2.5, texas
+0.6 ± 11.2 (budget 1/class). Across all 9 graphs the gain falls as
homophily rises — Spearman ρ = −0.550, exactly the preregistered threshold,
but p = 0.125 at n = 9 (directional support, underpowered):

## 6. What the day settles, and what it opens

**Settled:** the baseline is valid and its gap to published numbers is
explained; the semantic view's value is graph-dependent with a homophily
trend in the predicted direction; concat is the best of the three fusions
tested; the HSIC machinery (gate and penalty) is inert as configured; the
pubmed explanation gain is leakage; e5's failure is fusion amplification of
a small embedding gap.

**Opened (next preregistrations):**
1. A heterophily-appropriate backbone (H2GCN-class) under the same arms —
   the current bottleneck on heterophilic graphs is the backbone, not the view.
2. shared_private v2 after a post-mortem of v1's instability.
3. A leakage-robust explanation prompt (no category list) or post-cutoff
   data, if the explanation view is to survive.
4. Gate recalibration only matters if a fusion choice ever beats always-concat;
   today nothing did.

**Limitations:** fixed shared hyperparameters (absolute accuracies below
published CG3 by design); citeseer_tag is the 3,186-node TAG subset (not
comparable to published CiteSeer); tiny-graph budgets of 1–4/class give
±7–11 pt per-graph CIs; heterophily correlation is n = 9; Planetoid-family
datasets are heavily reproduced in LLM training data (contamination caveat
applies to all LLM-derived views).

**Ledger:** ~$8–10 of Modal compute today across V1 (3 jobs), FEW-30
(3), FEW-31 (4). Every run's log and MLflow db is a project artifact;
verdicts live on Linear FEW-30/FEW-31 and in the two hypotheses docs.
